Questions

Where are my notes stored?

In your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. They don’t sync between devices, and clearing your browser’s site data removes them. Deleted notes stay in the Recycle bin for 30 days.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Fact check, Sourcing, Study and the writing tools only work if you add a Groq key to js/keys.js, and “Turn off all AI features” in AI settings hides them completely. Autocorrect and word prediction run on your device.

How accurate are the verdicts and prices?

They are AI-generated and the model has no live web access, so treat them as a strong first pass. The Sourcing advisor reads real store listings through the Verity server (run python3 server.py), but prices change often, so open the listing and confirm before you buy.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start writing.

Does it work offline?

Writing, searching and reviewing flashcards work once the page has loaded. AI tools need an internet connection.
