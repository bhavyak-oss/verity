AI settings

Switch AI features on or off. Autocorrect and word prediction work without AI.

Verity uses the Groq keys listed in js/keys.js (it tries the next key if one hits its limit) and the model openai/gpt-oss-120b. Nothing is typed or stored here. Verity sends nearby writing context to Groq for ghost-text suggestions, checks completed words for obvious typos, and sends selected text or a note when you use an AI writing action.

Share note

Opens your browser's print window. Choose “Save as PDF”, then send the file anywhere.

The link contains the note itself, so nothing is uploaded anywhere. People who open it get a read-only copy and can save it to their own notes. Later edits you make are not sent to them; share again to send a new version.
